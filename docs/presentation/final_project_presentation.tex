                        \item \textbf{Objective 2 (Method 2):} Extract aspect sentiment and topic drivers from 29,189 comments as leading indicators 3--7 days prior.                                  
                    \end{itemize}

                        \item \textbf{Proactive Pre-Allocation:} Pre-allocate retail inventory across tier-1 regional warehouses 7--10 days before peak consumer demand realization.                   
                        \item \textbf{Dynamic Ad Timing:} Synchronize marketing ad blitzes with positive sentiment velocity while throttling spend on negative-sentiment products.
